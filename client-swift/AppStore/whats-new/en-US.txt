PocketADM 2.0 is a new app: rebuilt from the ground up as a native iPhone app — with a new icon.

• Server overview: domains and where they lead, services and timers with their journal and start, stop and restart, cron jobs, Docker stacks and drives. PocketADM finds all of it on any server by itself.
• Themes: Midnight (OLED), Ocean, Aurora, Forest, Sunset, Terminal, Nord, Paper, Arctic and Rosé, plus the iOS look in light or dark.
• The assistant knows your server: it reads PocketADM's own records (updates and how they ended, activity, disk and memory over time, health) and keeps notes by topic that you can pin, edit and have tidied up.
• Attachments in the chat: photos, screenshots and files from the iPhone, or files, folders, containers with their logs, the server overview and health findings from the server.
• Every answer says which model wrote it. For Mistral Vibe you pick the model, including GLM and Mistral Large through your API key.
• Connecting a Claude subscription is more reliable: a clear message when a code does not fit, or paste a token from `claude setup-token`.
• The watch, as a chat, with notifications on the iPhone. The assistant keeps working when you leave the app.
• Files like an editor: edit with undo, upload, move, unpack, change permissions.
• Updates say what changes. Activity, Health and Apps are more compact.

The newest features need PocketADM 0.26 on the server: run the installer again to update it.
