PocketADM 2.0 is a new app: rebuilt from the ground up as a native iPhone app.

• Native from end to end: faster screens, a real terminal emulator, live charts.
• Pair in seconds: the server installer now ends with a QR code. Scan it and you are signed in, over HTTPS from the first minute, with no domain needed.
• Coding agents on your server: run Claude Code or Codex from the Assistant tab with your own subscription. Every step is shown, and the risky ones wait for your tap.
• Sign in with Authentik, Authelia, Keycloak or any OpenID Connect provider, next to password and 2FA.
• A more careful agent: anything that would talk to the internet asks first, and background monitors can only look.

Coming from 1.x? Sign in to your server once more, or scan its pairing QR. The newest features need PocketADM 0.23 on the server: run the installer again to update it.
