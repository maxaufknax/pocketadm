PocketADM 2.0 is a new app: rebuilt from the ground up as a native iPhone app.

• The watch: an AI looks at your server every few hours and right after anything breaks, and writes to you only when it is worth knowing — in plain sentences, with the next step one tap away. Quiet at night, never more than a few messages a day, and optionally as push notifications or in Element (Matrix).
• Your AI subscriptions in one place: connect Claude, ChatGPT and Mistral with a tap, no terminal — and choose which AI chats, watches and explains.
• Apps instead of container lists: containers that belong together appear as one app, each with a name you can tell apart. Container pages with live usage, a shell, processes, history and live logs.
• Health with a score, plain explanations and one-tap fixes — and a way to accept what is intentional.
• Files and drives: every folder, external disks included, with how full they are, folder sizes, search and previews.
• Live activity: containers, SSH logins, packages, internet outages and everything that happens in PocketADM.
• The assistant folds runs of commands into one line, keeps its plan in view, and chats can be searched, pinned, renamed and shared.
• Updates explain themselves: the installed and the new version, what changes, and what is briefly down while it updates.

The newest features need PocketADM 0.24 on the server: run the installer again to update it.
