PocketADM 2.0 is a new app: rebuilt from the ground up as a native iPhone app — with a new icon.

• The watch, as a chat: an AI keeps an eye on your server and sends short messages when something is worth knowing — details on a tap, the next step as a button. Ask it anything ("why is the disk so full?") or tell it what to stay quiet about. Its messages arrive as notifications.
• The assistant stays with you: leave the app and the session keeps running on the server; come back and everything is there, a pending approval included. When it waits for your OK, you get a notification.
• Tables, headings, lists and code look right in the chat.
• Files like an editor: start at "/", folders unfold in place, drives on top. Edit text files (with undo), upload photos and files, rename, move, copy, unpack, download as a zip, change permissions, delete.
• Updates say what changes: only what is new between your version and the next one, short, with the risk and a recommendation.
• Activity in two halves: what happens on the server, and what was done in PocketADM.
• A more compact Health: the score, the areas at a glance, the fix right on the row.
• Your AI subscriptions in one place: connect Claude, ChatGPT and Mistral with a tap, no terminal.
• Apps instead of container lists; container pages with live usage, a shell, processes and live logs.

The newest features need PocketADM 0.25 on the server: run the installer again to update it.
